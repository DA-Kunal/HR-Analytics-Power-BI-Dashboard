# HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee demographics, workforce distribution, and key attrition drivers across different departments and salary slabs.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview
> ![Dashboard Overview](HR%20dashboard-overview.png)

## 💼 Business Requirements
* **Identify Attrition Drivers:** Determine why employees are leaving by analyzing correlations between salary slabs, job roles, satisfaction scores, and department types.
* **Targeted Retention Strategies:** Highlight vulnerable segments (e.g., specific age groups or experience levels like early-career professionals) to deploy targeted retention programs.
* **Workforce Capacity Planning:** Monitor active versus total headcounts across departments (Operations, Sales, IT, etc.) to optimize hiring pipelines and resource allocation.
* **Compensation Benchmarking:** Review turnover rates within specific salary brackets (e.g., 0–3 LPA vs. higher brackets) to evaluate and adjust competitive pay structures.

## 🚀 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Employees:** 1.417K
* **Active Employees:** 1.186K
* **Attrition Count:** 231
* **Attrition Rate:** 16.30%
* **Average Age:** 36.94 years
* **Average Experience:** 7.04 years

## 🔍 Key Insights & Findings
* **Department Attrition:** Highest attrition is concentrated in Administration (36%) and Sales (23%).
* **Salary Slab Impact:** Employees in the 6–10 LPA salary range experience the highest volume of turnover, followed by the 0–3 LPA bracket.
* **Job Role & Satisfaction:** Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives show higher total attrition coupled with lower satisfaction score ratings (levels 1-2).
* **Experience Trends:** Peak turnover occurs around early tenure milestones (around 2 to 5 years of experience).
